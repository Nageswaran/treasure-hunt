Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Magician's Vanishing Trick

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 70-90 minutes
Tonight is opening night at the Grand Alcazar Theatre, and the star magician's signature finale — the Vanishing Cabinet trick — has gone completely wrong. The secret notes explaining how the trick works have been scattered, coded, and mixed up backstage, and the show starts in two hours. The theater manager has called you in personally. Solve all ten backstage mysteries, and the final page will reveal the one quality every great magician needs most.
MISSION 01
EASY

The Name Behind the Name

A dusty photo backstage shows the theater's very first headline act: the legendary escape artist Harry Houdini. But "Harry Houdini" was a stage name. Search online to find the real name he was born with.

Type his real first and last name together.

Hint 1

Search "Harry Houdini real name born." He was born in Budapest in 1874.

MISSION 02
EASY

The Scarf Trick

Backstage, someone left a practice sheet showing the order scarves get pulled from a sleeve, but the last one is smudged out:

🟡 🟡 🔴 🟡 🟡 🔴 🟡 🟡 ❓

What color comes next?

Hint 1

Look for a small group of colors that keeps repeating from the start.

💡 Did you know? Harry Houdini died on Halloween — October 31, 1926 — and his fans still hold séances every year on that date, hoping to hear from him.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

The Box Office Puzzle

The box office has two deals for a family of 4 tickets. Deal A: tickets cost $15 each, and buy-3-get-the-4th-free. Deal B: a flat "family pass" for exactly 4 tickets costs $50. Work out the total cost of each deal, figure out which is cheaper, then answer: how many dollars would the family save by choosing the cheaper deal instead of the other one?

Agent's notebook: while checking the price board, you spot a keypad code taped underneath it, for the backstage door: 7. Worth remembering.

Hint 1

For Deal A, remember the 4th ticket is free — you only pay for 3 of them.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

The Vanishing Cabinet's Blueprint

The old blueprint shows the front panel of the Vanishing Cabinet: a rectangle topped with a triangular peak. The carpenter needs to know the total area of the front panel (in square meters) to order the right amount of velvet fabric to cover it.

The triangle's base is exactly as wide as the rectangle below it. The cabinet has a solid wood floor with a hidden trapdoor built in — no mirrors anywhere inside it.
Hint 1

Work out the rectangle's area and the triangle's area separately, then add them.

Hint 2

A triangle's area is half of its base times its height.

MEDIUM

Hint 1

Replace each emoji with its matching letter, in order, and read the result.

MISSION 06
HARD

Two Assistants, One Key

Backstage there are two assistants: one always tells the truth, and the other always lies — but you don't know which is which yet. The sign-in sheet clearly shows Assistant A arrived at 6:00 PM. But Assistant A just told you, "I arrived at 5:00 PM."

Assistant B then tells you, "The key to the cabinet is hidden inside the Blue box."

Which box is the key really in — Red or Blue?

Hint 1

The sign-in sheet is a fact you can trust. Compare it to what Assistant A said — does that make A the truth-teller or the liar?

Hint 2

Once you know Assistant A is lying, you know exactly what Assistant B must be — and that tells you whether to trust what B said about the box.

MISSION 07
HARD

De of Het Achter het Gordijn

The old Dutch theater program lists four words: goochelaar (magician), truc (trick), hoed (hat), and konijn (rabbit). Three of these words use "de," and one uses "het." Which one is the odd one out — the "het" word?

Hint 1

Try saying "de ___" and "het ___" out loud for each word — one will sound clearly wrong with "de."

💡 Did you know? Trapdoors have been used to make performers "vanish" through the stage floor since long before Houdini — theaters were using them as far back as Shakespeare's time, over 400 years ago!

MISSION 08
HARD

The Founding Year

Stage 1 of 2

Search online to find the year Harry Houdini died. The Grand Alcazar Theatre (where you are now) first opened its doors in 1889. How many years had the theater already been open when Houdini died?

Hint 1

Search "what year did Houdini die," then subtract 1889 from that year.

Stage 2 of 2

Take your Stage 1 answer. Subtract the backstage keypad code you noted a few missions back. Then double whatever is left. What's the final number?

Hint 1

What number did your notebook say was taped under the price board back at the box office?

MISSION 09
AHA!

Two Witnesses, One Truth

Two audience members are arguing about how the Vanishing Cabinet trick works. One says, "She must have slipped out through a trapdoor in the floor." The other says, "No, it was done with a hidden mirror reflecting the back wall."

Think back to the cabinet's blueprint from Mission 04 — what did it actually show inside the cabinet? You can also quickly search whether trapdoors or mirrors have the longer, better-documented history for floor-vanishing tricks, to double-check your reasoning.

Which explanation is correct: the trapdoor, or the mirror?

Hint 1

Scroll back and re-read the caption under Mission 04's diagram carefully.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

The Secret Order

The trick only works if 4 differently colored scarves are loaded into the cabinet in one exact secret order. No two scarves can share a spot, and every scarf must be used exactly once. How many different possible orders are there for loading all 4 scarves?

Hint 1

Think about it one scarf at a time: how many choices for the first spot? Then how many are left for the second spot?

Hint 2

Multiply your choices for each spot together: 4 choices, then 3, then 2, then 1.
