Mission 07 · Hard

De of Het Achter het Gordijn

Answer: konijn
How to get there

"De goochelaar," "de truc," and "de hoed" all use "de." "Konijn" (rabbit) is the exception: it's "het konijn."

Why this is the only answer

Dutch grammatical gender is fixed per word; among these four, only "konijn" is a "het" word.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search against Dutch dictionary sources (Woorden.org, ANW, Cambridge Dutch-English Dictionary) for each of the four words individually.

Mission 08 · Hard (multi-stage)

The Founding Year

Stage 1 answer: 37
Stage 2 (final) answer: 60
How to get there

Stage 2: Subtract the keypad code planted in Mission 03 (7): 37 − 7 = 30. Double it: 30 × 2 = 60.

Why this is the only answer

Houdini's death year (1926) is a well-documented historical fact, and the theater's founding year is a fixed fictional detail stated in-story, making Stage 1 a single forced number; Stage 2's arithmetic is likewise single-valued.
