Mission 01 · Easy · Dutch Language

The Guild's Word Test

Answer: goud
How to get there

All six words are workshop materials. Correct articles: de veer, de spiegel, de naald, de zijde, het goud, de lijm. Only "goud" (gold) actually takes "het" — metals and many raw materials/substances in Dutch are neuter ("het goud," like "het zilver" and "het ijzer"). The other five genuinely take "de," so "goud" is the one written wrongly on the list.

Why this is the only answer

Every other word on the list correctly uses "de" — only "goud" is mismatched with the article shown. The child should type just the word "goud," not "de goud" or "het goud."

Source / fact used

Standard Dutch grammar (de/het word classification) — not a web-verified historical fact, but confirmed core vocabulary knowledge (metals/materials as neuter "het" nouns, e.g. het goud, het zilver, het ijzer).

Mission 02 · Easy · Logical Reasoning (Foundational — classification)

Sorting the Workshop Shelf

Answer: Colombina
How to get there

Four masks (Bauta, Volto, Zanni, Medico della Peste) are each described as covering "the whole face." Colombina is described as covering "only the area around the eyes and nose," leaving the mouth and chin free. It's the only half-face mask in the group, so it doesn't belong with the other four.

Why this is the only answer

The puzzle is solvable purely from the four given clues — no outside lookup needed. Every other mask's card explicitly says "whole face," so only Colombina can be the odd one out.

Source / fact used

Real-world basis, verified via web search: the Colombina is a half-mask covering the eyes/nose/upper cheeks, while the Bauta, Volto, Zanni, and Medico della Peste are all traditionally full-face masks (sources: veneziamaschere.com, visit.venice.it, lauramorelli.com).

This is the single most commonly cited defining feature of the Bauta across every source — accept "mouth," "a mouth," "mouth hole," or "mouthpiece" as equivalent if the child phrases it slightly differently; the puzzle instructs them to type just one word to keep this simple.

Mission 04 · Medium · Geography & World Knowledge

Elena's Neighborhood

Answer: San Polo
How to get there

Venice is divided into six sestieri: San Marco, San Polo, Santa Croce, Dorsoduro, Cannaregio, and Castello. Searching for "smallest sestiere of Venice" or a list of the six with descriptions identifies San Polo as both the smallest and one of the oldest, centered right by the Rialto Bridge.

Why this is the only answer

Only one sestiere is described as both smallest AND established near the Rialto Bridge — the two clues together point uniquely to San Polo (Dorsoduro and Cannaregio are notably larger; Castello is the largest).

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: "San Polo is Venice's smallest district and one of the oldest in the city, established near the Rialto Bridge" (caffeflorian.com and corroborating sestieri guides).

Mission 05 · Medium · Mathematics (geometry / measurement, self-drawn diagram)

Gold Leaf for the Regina d'Oro

Answer: 148 (square cm)
How to get there

Big rectangle area = 16 cm × 10 cm = 160 cm². Each eye hole = 3 cm × 2 cm = 6 cm². Two eye holes = 12 cm². Gold-leaf area = 160 − 12 = 148 cm².

Why this is the only answer

Every dimension needed is labeled directly on the diagram, and the SVG coordinates were drawn to match exactly: outer rectangle 240×150 px at a 15px-per-cm scale (16×10 cm), two eye-hole rectangles each 45×30 px (3×2 cm) placed symmetrically, so the picture and the math agree.

Source / fact used

Self-contained pen-and-paper geometry puzzle (composite-shape subtraction); recomputed independently to confirm 160 − 12 = 148.

Mission 06 · Hard · History & Civilizations + Critical Thinking / Media Literacy

Two Websites Disagree

Answer: 1436
How to get there

Searching "Mascareri guild statute Venice" confirms that the mask-makers (mascareri) of Venice were officially recognized as their own craft with their own statute dated 10 April 1436 (they were regulated alongside the painters' guild, but with their own dedicated statute from that date).

Why this is the only answer

1650 (Site B) is a fabricated distractor with no historical basis for this guild's founding statute — real sources consistently cite 1436, and several even give the exact date, 10 April 1436.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search across multiple sources (imagesofvenice.com, italymask.co.nz, and others): "the mascherari would be considered as painters... with their own laws, their own guild and their own statute since April 10, 1436."

Four Apprentices, Four Masks

Answer: Giulia
How to get there

Every apprentice and mask is uniquely assigned by chaining the six clues through elimination — no branch requires guessing, and no other assignment satisfies all six clues simultaneously (verified by working the full grid: Marco-Bauta-Gold, Luca-Volto-Ivory, Beatrice-Colombina-Silver, Giulia-Moretta-Black).

Mission 08 · Hard · Financial Literacy + Mathematics (pen-and-paper)

The Glassmaker's Bargain

Answer: B
How to get there

Deal A: €48 × 0.90 (10% off) = €43.20 for 60 beads → €43.20 ÷ 60 = €0.72 per bead.
Deal B: €68 for 100 beads, no discount → €68 ÷ 100 = €0.68 per bead.
€0.68 is less than €0.72, so Deal B is the better value per bead.

Why this is the only answer

The numbers were chosen so both divisions come out exact (no rounding ambiguity: 43.2 ÷ 60 = 0.72 exactly, 68 ÷ 100 = 0.68 exactly), so there's only one correct comparison and one correct letter to type.

Source / fact used

Self-contained pen-and-paper percentage/unit-price puzzle; recomputed independently to confirm €0.72 vs €0.68.

Mission 09 · Big "Aha!" · Logical Reasoning (Advanced — working backward + combinatorics), Multi-Stage

Elena's Paint Shelf

Stage 1 answer: 16

How to get there (Stage 1)

Work backward from the end state. She ended with 5 bottles after giving away 3, so before giving those away she had 5 + 3 = 8 bottles. Those 8 bottles were "half" of her starting amount (after using the other half on the mask), so her starting amount was 8 × 2 = 16.

Why this is the only answer (Stage 1)

Check forward: start with 16 → use half (8) on the mask → 8 remain → give 3 to Giulia → 5 remain. This matches the story exactly, and each step only has one possible reverse operation, so 16 is the unique solution.

Stage 2 (final) answer: 120

How to get there (Stage 2)

Using Stage 1's result (16 colors), the number of ways to choose 2 colors from 16 (order doesn't matter) is the combinations formula n × (n − 1) ÷ 2 = 16 × 15 ÷ 2 = 240 ÷ 2 = 120.

Why this is the only answer (Stage 2)

This mission is deliberately dependent: Stage 2 cannot be solved without Stage 1's exact number 16 as the input n. Recomputed independently: 16 × 15 = 240, 240 ÷ 2 = 120.

Source / fact used

Self-contained working-backward and combinatorics puzzle chain — solvable purely by reasoning, no outside lookup required at either stage.

The Guild's Secret Code

Answer: creativity
How to get there

Why this is the only answer

A Caesar/shift cipher with a fixed, known shift amount has exactly one decoding. The shift (7) and ciphertext were both re-derived and re-encoded independently to confirm the round-trip: CREATIVITY shifted forward by 7 produces exactly JYLHAPCPAF, so shifting the given ciphertext backward by 7 must reproduce CREATIVITY.
