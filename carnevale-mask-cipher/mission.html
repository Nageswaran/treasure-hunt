Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Case of the Vanished Mask Pattern

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 75-100 minutes
Signora Elena Contarini is Venice's most celebrated mascareri — a mask maker. Tonight is the Volo dell'Angelo, the ceremony that opens Carnevale, and her masterpiece mask, the "Regina d'Oro," is meant to lead the parade. But the secret pattern needed to finish it has vanished from her workshop. Elena has called the Internet Detective Academy. Follow the clues through Venice's canals, guild history, and a centuries-old code to recover the pattern before the parade begins.
MISSION 01
EASY

The Guild's Word Test

Elena's apprentice, Marco, won't let just anyone into the workshop. "Every detective who trains with the Academy proves it first," he says, and hands you a list of workshop words in Dutch — each one written with "de" in front.

de veer · de spiegel · de naald · de zijde · de goud · de lijm

Five of these words are correctly paired with "de." One of them is wrong — it should really use "het" instead. Which word is the odd one out? Type just that one word (no "de" or "het" in front).

Hint 1

Say each phrase out loud. Materials and metals — like silver, iron, or this one — often prefer "het" instead of "de."

MISSION 02
EASY

Sorting the Workshop Shelf

Marco lets you in. On a shelf, five unfinished masks wait for their ribbons. A card sits under each one:

Bauta — hides the whole face, from chin to forehead.
Volto — hides the whole face, smooth and pale.
Zanni — hides the whole face, with a long beak-like nose.
Medico della Peste — hides the whole face, with a long beak-like nose.
Colombina — hides only the area around the eyes and nose, leaving the mouth and chin free.

Four of these masks cover the same amount of face. One does not belong with the others. Which mask is it?

Hint 1

Read each card again and compare how MUCH of the face each mask covers, not what it looks like.

💡 Did you know? Napoleon banned Venice's Carnevale in 1797, afraid that masked crowds could plot against him in secret. It wasn't officially celebrated again until 1979 — almost 200 years later!

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

The Photograph on the Wall

🕵️ Agent's Notebook: As you step inside the workshop, you notice a small brass plate above the door: "Bottega No. 7." Numbers like this are always worth remembering.

Pinned above Elena's workbench is a real photograph of Venetian Carnival masks. Look for the plainest, palest style — the one called the Bauta.

A real photo of Venetian Carnival masks on display in Venice.

Search online to learn about the Bauta mask. It's famous for something it does NOT have — a missing facial feature that let its wearer eat, drink, and talk all night without ever taking the mask off. Type just that one missing feature (one word).

Hint 1

Search "Bauta mask Venice" and read how people could still eat and talk while wearing it.

Hint 2

Try the exact search "Bauta mask no mouth" and read the first good result carefully.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Elena's Neighborhood

Elena's old guild ledger says her workshop stands in Venice's smallest sestiere — a sestiere is one of Venice's six historic districts. Her sestiere is also one of the very oldest parts of the city, built up right beside the Rialto Bridge.

Search online to find the name of this sestiere. Type its name.

Hint 1

Venice has six sestieri in total. Look up a map or list of all six and where each one sits.

Hint 2

Search "smallest sestiere of Venice" directly.

MISSION 05
MEDIUM

Gold Leaf for the Regina d'Oro

Before she can cut a new pattern, Elena needs to know how much gold leaf to order. Here is the flat mask shape she plans to cover — every measurement you need is labeled on the drawing. Both eye holes are exactly the same size.

Find the area of gold leaf needed — the whole mask shape, minus BOTH eye holes. Answer in square centimeters (just the number).
Hint 1

First find the area of the big rectangle. Then find the area of ONE eye hole and double it.

Hint 2

Big rectangle: 16 × 10. One eye hole: 3 × 2. Subtract both eye holes from the big rectangle.

💡 Did you know? Venetian women once wore a small oval mask called the Moretta, held in place only by biting a button between their teeth. That made talking impossible — so it earned the nickname "the mute mask"!

MISSION 06
HARD

Two Websites Disagree

Elena wants to know exactly how old her craft's traditions really are. You find two websites that disagree about the mask-makers' guild, called the Mascareri.

Site A says: "The mask-makers of Venice received their own official guild statute in 1436."
Site B says: "The mask-makers of Venice received their own official guild statute in 1650."

Search online to find out which year real historical sources confirm. Type the correct year (just the number).

Hint 1

Search "Mascareri guild statute Venice" and check when their craft was first officially recognized with its own rules.

Hint 2

Real sources often name an exact day and month too, in April of that year.

💡 Did you know? Murano and Burano, two small islands in the Venice lagoon, are famous for two totally different crafts — Murano for colorful blown glass, and Burano for delicate handmade lace.

MISSION 07
HARD

Four Apprentices, Four Masks

Four apprentices — Giulia, Marco, Beatrice, and Luca — are racing to finish four masks — Bauta, Volto, Colombina, and Moretta — each in its own color, before tonight. Use these clues:

1. Giulia is not making the Bauta. Giulia is not making the Volto either.
2. The Moretta will be painted black.
3. Marco's mask will be gold. Marco is not making the Colombina.
4. Beatrice is not making the Moretta.
5. The Colombina will be silver.
6. Luca is making the Volto.

Which apprentice's mask will be painted black?

Hint 1

Start with the clue that names someone directly, like Luca's mask. Then use the other clues to rule out options one by one.

Hint 2

You know Marco's mask is gold. Which mask does another clue say is a different color? That mask can't be Marco's.

MISSION 08
HARD

The Glassmaker's Bargain

Grab a pencil for this one. On Murano, two glassmakers sell the tiny glass beads Elena needs to decorate the Regina d'Oro:

Deal A: 60 beads for €48, with a 10% discount if bought today.
Deal B: 100 beads for €68, no discount.

Work out the price PER BEAD for each deal, after any discount. Which deal gives the lower price per bead — A or B? Type just the letter.

Hint 1

First find each deal's total price AFTER any discount is subtracted. Deal B has no discount to apply.

Hint 2

Then divide each deal's discounted total price by its number of beads, to get the price for just one bead.

MISSION 09
AHA!

Elena's Paint Shelf

Stage 1 of 2

Elena suddenly remembers something. "I used exactly HALF of my pigment bottles finishing the mask," she says. "Then I gave 3 bottles to my apprentice Giulia. After that, I had exactly 5 bottles left."

How many pigment bottles did Elena start with?

Hint 1

Work backwards from the end. Start at 5 bottles, and undo each step in reverse order.

Stage 2 of 2

Now use the number you just found — that's how many different pigment colors Elena started with. The guild seal needs exactly 2 different colors mixed together for the Regina d'Oro's signature shade.

How many different 2-color combinations are possible, if the order doesn't matter (Gold+Silver is the same pair as Silver+Gold)?

Hint 1

This is a combinations problem, not a permutations problem — picking Gold-then-Silver is the same pair as Silver-then-Gold, so don't count it twice.

Hint 2

The number of ways to choose 2 items from a group of n is n × (n − 1) ÷ 2. Plug in the number from Stage 1.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

The Guild's Secret Code

Behind a loose floorboard, you find the missing pattern — folded around a strip of parchment covered in strange letters. A note explains: the Mascareri guild always protected its secrets with a shift cipher. Every real letter was moved forward through the alphabet by a fixed number of places to hide it.

You need that number. Then think back to your very first minutes inside Elena's workshop — a small brass plate held the number the guild has always used as its key.

Shift each letter of this message BACKWARD by that many places to reveal what your detective work has earned:

JYLHAPCPAF

Hint 1

A shift cipher (also called a Caesar cipher) moves every letter the same number of steps through the alphabet: A, B, C, D... To undo it, shift each letter backward by that same number.

Hint 2

Not sure of the number? Scroll back to Mission 3, the moment you first stepped inside the workshop — a brass plate above the door held it.

Case closed?

The pattern is safe, and Elena's Regina d'Oro will lead tonight's parade. But the real treasure was how you cracked this case — by mixing language, maps, math, logic, and code-breaking into one clever chain of clues. That's creativity at work, Agent — well done.
