Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Silent Sensor of Baron 1898

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 80-100 minutes

Tomorrow, Efteling reopens its real roller coaster Baron 1898 after routine maintenance — but tonight, the safety sensor line along the track has gone completely silent. You've been called in as a Junior Ride-Safety Apprentice to work through the maintenance logs before the morning test-run. Solve all ten logs, and you'll uncover the quality that turned this stressful night into the engineering team's finest work.

MISSION 01
EASY

The Control Panel Light

Before every safe launch, the control panel's warning light blinks a countdown pattern: 2, 4, 8, 16, __. What number comes next?

Hint 1

Look at what's happening between each number and the next one — it's the same operation every time.

💡 Did you know? Efteling opened in 1952 in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands — making it Europe's oldest theme park, opening three full years before Disneyland.

MISSION 02
EASY

Bram's Dutch Maintenance Note

Head engineer Bram left a note in Dutch: "Controleer ___ trein voor vertrek" (Check the train before departure). Is it "de trein" or "het trein" in Dutch? Type just the correct little word: de or het.

Hint 1

Search a Dutch dictionary or translator for "trein" and check which little word comes before it.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

Measuring the Loop

One of the track's loop-shaped inversions is drawn on the blueprint below as a near-perfect circle. Using the diameter marked on the diagram, calculate the loop's circumference in meters, rounded to the nearest whole number. Use π ≈ 3.14.

Circumference of a circle = π × diameter.
Hint 1

Circumference is just π times the diameter — no need to find the radius first.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Two Conflicting Logs

Two maintenance logs about last night can't BOTH be true. Bram's log says: "I ran the full sensor diagnostic alone from 9:00 PM to 9:45 PM, then locked the control room and went straight home — nobody else had a key." Els's log says: "I saw Bram leave the park at 8:30 PM. I used my spare key and ran a diagnostic myself from 9:00 to 9:45 PM."

The park's exit-gate scanner confirms Bram's badge scanned OUT at 8:32 PM. Whose log actually matches what happened — Bram's or Els's?

Hint 1

Compare the gate-scanner time to the time Bram's log claims he was still inside running the diagnostic.

💡 Did you know? After the lift hill, roller coasters don't use an engine to move — gravity and stored energy do all the work for the rest of the ride.

📓 Agent's Notebook: Bram notes this password in the logbook margin as tonight's session code. Jot it down, you'll need it again later.

MISSION 06
HARD

Racing the Safety Zone

Stage 1 of 2

✏️ Grab a pencil for this one. Search the internet: what is Baron 1898's real top speed, in km/h? Convert that speed into meters per second. (Tip: divide km/h by 3.6.)

Hint 1

Search "Baron 1898 top speed km/h." Once you have the number, divide it by 3.6 to get meters per second.

Stage 2 of 2

The new sensor needs to detect the train crossing a 100-meter safety zone. Using your speed in meters per second from Stage 1, how many SECONDS does the train take to cross the full 100-meter zone?

Hint 1

Time = distance ÷ speed.

MISSION 07
HARD

The Gift Shop Model Train

The gift shop sells a model of the Baron 1898 train for €25, currently marked 20% off. An online toy store sells the exact same model for €22, with no discount. After applying the gift shop's discount, how many EUROS cheaper is the gift shop's price compared to the online store? Type just the number.

Hint 1

First find 20% of €25 and subtract it from €25 to get the gift shop's real price. Then compare that to €22.

MISSION 08
HARD

Working Backward From the Last Log

Search the internet: how many SECONDS does a full ride on Baron 1898 really last? The sensor's final log shows the train EXITED the ride at exactly 9:47:15 PM, having spent that many seconds on the full ride, start to finish. Working backward, what time did the train LEAVE the station at the very start of the ride? Type your answer in this exact format: 9:45 PM

Hint 1

Search "Baron 1898 ride duration seconds." Convert that number of seconds into minutes and seconds, then subtract from the exit time.

Hint 2

60 seconds = 1 minute. However many whole minutes fit into the ride time, subtract that many minutes (and any leftover seconds) from 9:47:15 PM.

MISSION 09
HARD

The Aha! Moment — Below Ground Level

Search the internet: what is Baron 1898's real height (its highest point above the ground, in meters) AND its real first-drop height (in meters)? A drop can't be longer than the height it starts from... unless part of the track dips BELOW ground level, into a trench! Using the two real numbers you found, how many meters below ground does the track dip?

Hint 1

Search "Baron 1898 height" and "Baron 1898 first drop." Subtract the height from the drop.

Hint 2

If the drop is bigger than the height, the extra distance has to go somewhere — straight down, past ground level.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

Restarting the Sensor Line

The control room's final lock needs tonight's session password from Mission 5 — but typed BACKWARD, letter by letter.

Take the password you decoded earlier, reverse the order of its letters, and type the result.

Hint 1

Scroll back to Mission 5's Agent's Notebook if you need the password again.

Hint 2

Write the password out, then read it from the last letter to the first.

Case closed?

Every sensor light on the panel glows green — Baron 1898 is ready for tomorrow's guests. It took real creativity to piece together clues from blueprints, logs, and old ciphers into one working answer. Well done, Agent.
