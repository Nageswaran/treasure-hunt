How to get there

Why this is the only answer

The scaling factor (30/8) is fixed by the two serving counts given, and multiplying the one ingredient asked about (butter) by that factor gives a single exact value.

How to get there

Grand Finale · Stage 1 of 2

The Golden Sponge Showdown

Answer: 2198
How to get there

The oven temperature planted (and flagged "jot this down") back in Mission 06's notebook, Page 2 Line 1, is "one hundred and seventy five degrees Celsius" = 175°C. Grandma's rule: height (cm) = temperature ÷ 25 = 175 ÷ 25 = 7 cm. The diagram labels the diameter as 20 cm, so the radius = 10 cm. Cylinder volume = π × r² × h = 3.14 × 10 × 10 × 7 = 3.14 × 700 = 2198 cm³ exactly (no rounding needed with π ≈ 3.14).

Why this is the only answer

Every number feeding the formula is fixed: the diameter is labeled on the diagram, the height is fixed by Grandma's stated rule combined with the one specific temperature planted in Mission 06, and the volume formula and π value are both given in the puzzle text — so the calculation has exactly one result.

Source / fact used

The 20 cm tin diameter reflects the real, verified tradition that a Victoria sponge is baked in two 20 cm (8-inch) round sandwich tins — confirmed across multiple published recipes (Mary Berry, the National Trust, and The Great British Bake Off official recipe pages all specify 20 cm/8-inch tins). The 175°C figure and the "divide by 25" rule are original details invented for this mission's story and cipher.
