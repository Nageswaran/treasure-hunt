The Golden Bakery Mystery

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 75-95 minutes

Golden Crust Bakery, in the little town of Willowbrook, is famous for one thing: a prize-winning Golden Victoria Sponge, baked from a secret recipe in Grandma Rosa's old notebook. Two days before the Willowbrook Grand Baking Fair, the notebook has gone missing — and a strange email claiming the bakery just "won" a $5,000 sponsorship deal has landed in the inbox. Something isn't right. Owner Elena has called in a junior food-safety-and-recipe detective to find the notebook, protect the bakery from getting scammed, and get a real Golden Victoria Sponge onto the judges' table in time. That detective is you.

MISSION 01
EASY

Psst — bakeries always list anything that could trigger an allergy, like the almonds above. Handy to notice, but not what this question asks.

Elena is double-checking her case labels before the judges arrive. Look at the Sugars row on both labels. How many more grams of sugar are in one Double Chocolate Muffin than in one Golden Honey Oat Muffin? Type just the number of grams, like 5.

Hint 1

Find the "Sugars" row on each label and look at the number right after it — then subtract the smaller one from the bigger one.

💡 Did you know? A sealed jar of honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still safe to eat, because honey has so little water in it — and it's naturally acidic — that bacteria simply can't grow in it.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

The Flour Deal Dilemma

With the fair so close, Golden Crust Bakery needs to order 100 kg of baking flour, right away. Flour only comes in 25 kg bags, and Elena has two supplier flyers pinned to the corkboard.

Golden Fields Milling Co.

25 kg bag of baking flour — just €18.00 per bag!

(Delivery fee: €4.00 per order, added at checkout)

Miller & Sons Wholesale

25 kg bag of baking flour — €19.50 per bag.

FREE delivery on every order!

Grab a pencil for this one. Work out the TOTAL cost for 100 kg of flour from each supplier — bags plus any delivery fee. How many euros cheaper is the better deal? Type just the number of euros, like 5.

Hint 1

Work out one supplier's total first, remembering to add the delivery fee if there is one, before you try the other.

Hint 2

100 kg means exactly 4 bags of 25 kg from either supplier — multiply the bag price by 4, then add the delivery fee if that supplier charges one.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Scaling Up for Thirty

A local school just ordered enough Golden Crust Everyday Sponge to serve 30 people at their spring assembly. Elena hands you the regular recipe card, which only serves 8.

Golden Crust Everyday Sponge (Serves 8)

  • 200 g butter, softened
  • 200 g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 4 tablespoons milk

Grab a pencil for this one — every ingredient has to grow by the exact same amount, or the sponge won't rise right. If 8 servings need 200 g of butter, how many grams of butter are needed for 30 servings? Type just the number of grams.

Hint 1

Figure out first how many times bigger 30 servings is than 8 servings — it isn't a whole number, so keep that pencil handy.

Hint 2

30 divided by 8 is 3.75. Multiply the butter amount by 3.75 to scale it up.

💡 Did you know? The Victoria sponge cake is named after Queen Victoria, who loved eating a slice with her afternoon tea. Traditionally it's filled with jam and just dusted with sugar on top — no icing at all!

MISSION 05
HARD

The Suspicious Sponsorship Email

Elena shows you the message that's had her worried all week. Golden Crust Bakery has ordered wholesale cocoa and sugar from TastyCorp Foods before — their real order confirmations always come from orders@tastycorpfoods.com. Here's what arrived in the inbox two days ago:

From: TastyCorp Rewards Team <rewards@tastycorp-bonus-mail.net>
🎉 YOU'RE A WINNER! $5,000 Golden Sponsorship — CLAIM WITHIN 24 HOURS!!

Dear Lucky Bakery Owner,

Golden Crust Bakery has been RANDOMLY SELECTED out of thousands of bakeries to win an exclusive $5,000 sponsorship deal from TastyCorp Foods! This incredible, once-in-a-lifetime opportunity will make your small bakery famous overnight!

To claim your prize TODAY, simply reply with your bakery's bank account number and the owner's date of birth so our accounting team can transfer the funds immediately.

This amazing offer expires in 24 hours — don't let it slip away! Click below now!

[ CLAIM MY $5,000 PRIZE ]

Warmly,
The TastyCorp Rewards Team

Real detectives check every suspicious message against four warning signs of a scam:

  1. It creates urgency ("act fast or you'll lose it")
  2. It's sent from an address that doesn't match the real company's website
  3. It asks for private information like bank details or a birthdate
  4. It promises something way too good to be true

Read the message above carefully. How many of these four warning signs actually appear in it? Type just the number.

Hint 1

Real companies always send mail from their real website's address. Compare the sender's email address above to the bakery's usual TastyCorp address.

Hint 2

Go through the four warning-sign categories one at a time, and check off each one you can actually find written in the message.

MISSION 06
HARD

Grandma Rosa's Coded Notebook

The notebook wasn't stolen after all — Elena finds it tucked inside an old flour bin. But paperclipped to the front is a strange note, just a string of number groups, in Grandma Rosa's handwriting. Here are the two pages the note seems to point to.

Page 1

  1. Every good baker starts the morning by warming the old brick oven slowly.
  2. Sift the flour twice so the golden sponge rises light and tall and even.
  3. Cream the butter with sugar until the mixture turns pale and fluffy soft.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time and beat them in very gently now.
  5. Fold in the flour with a wooden spoon never rushing never stirring hard.
  6. The secret truly is patience plus one spoonful of honey stirred in last.

Page 2

  1. Bake the golden sponge at one hundred and seventy five degrees Celsius exactly.
  2. Jot this down agent the oven must always stay at exactly that same temperature.
  3. Watch the cake through the little glass door but never open it early.
  4. When it springs back gently to a light touch the sponge is ready.
  5. Cool it fully on a wire rack before spreading the strawberry jam inside.
  6. Then dust the top with sugar and share it with someone you love.

📝 Some lines in a notebook matter more than others. Worth keeping in mind.

Grandma Rosa's note reads:

1-6-2 · 1-6-4 · 1-4-11 · 1-1-9 · 2-2-6 · 2-3-8

Each group of numbers means Page-Line-Word. Find the exact word in that spot on that page (count from word 1 at the start of each line) to decode her message, one word at a time. Type the six words in order, separated by spaces.

Hint 1

Book ciphers use three numbers: which page, then which line on that page, then which word in that line — start counting from word 1 at the start of each line.

Hint 2

Write out each of the six words you find, in order, on scratch paper — read them together as one short sentence when you're done.

💡 Did you know? The golden-brown color on baked bread and cakes comes from something called the Maillard reaction. It happens when heat makes sugars and proteins react together, creating hundreds of new toasty, caramel-like flavors — and that golden color bakers love.

MISSION 07
BIG AHA

The Flavor Combination Count

The fair's "Best Combo" contest lets Golden Crust Bakery enter exactly ONE flavor combination: one frosting plus one topping.

Frostings (4): Vanilla, Chocolate, Lemon, Caramel

Toppings (5): Sprinkles, Fresh Berries, Almond Flakes, Coconut Shavings, Sugar Pearls

Two contest rules ban specific pairings:

  1. Chocolate frosting can NOT be paired with Almond Flakes (another baker already entered that exact combo)
  2. Lemon frosting can NOT be paired with Coconut Shavings (the judges banned that combo after complaints last year)

Grab a pencil for this one. A grid with frostings across the top and toppings down the side makes it easy to count every pair systematically. How many different frosting-and-topping combinations could Golden Crust Bakery still choose from?

Hint 1

First count ALL possible frosting-and-topping pairs, completely ignoring the two banned combos — how many pairs is that in total?

Hint 2

Draw a grid: 4 frostings across the top, 5 toppings down the side, giving 20 boxes. Cross out the two banned boxes, then count every box that's left.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

The Golden Sponge Showdown

Stage 1 of 2

It's contest morning! Elena found one more line scrawled in the margin of Grandma Rosa's notebook: "Always fill the tin with batter until it stands as many centimeters tall as my favorite oven temperature, divided by 25." You noted that favorite temperature earlier in this very case — now's the moment it matters.

The round cake tin is drawn below. A cylinder's volume is found with: volume = π × radius × radius × height. Use π ≈ 3.14, and give your answer in whole cubic centimeters (round to the nearest whole number if needed).

Radius is half the diameter. Height comes from Grandma's rule above, not from the drawing.
Hint 1

Radius is HALF of the diameter shown in the drawing — and remember, the height comes from Grandma's rule, not from the picture.

Hint 2

Height = (the temperature you remembered) ÷ 25. Once you have the radius and the height, multiply: 3.14 × radius × radius × height.

Stage 2 of 2 — The Final Word

Add together every digit in your Stage 1 answer. If the result has two digits, add those digits together too, and keep going until only ONE digit is left — that's your shift number.

Now decode this note Elena taped to the display case, by shifting every letter BACKWARD through the alphabet by your shift number (so if your shift number were 1, B would become A, C would become B, and so on):

MKPFPGUU

Type the decoded word — it's the real prize this whole case was about.

Hint 1

Example: if your Stage 1 answer were 1234, you'd add 1+2+3+4 = 10, then 1+0 = 1 — keep adding digits until only one is left.

Hint 2

Shifting backward means each letter moves a few steps earlier in the alphabet. Try shifting just the first letter first, to check you've got the right number of steps.

Case closed?

Golden Crust Bakery didn't fall for the fake sponsorship, and the Golden Victoria Sponge made it to the judges' table after all. But the biggest win came after: Elena shared Grandma Rosa's recipe, and a table of warm samples, with the whole neighborhood. The final word you decoded is exactly what made that possible.
