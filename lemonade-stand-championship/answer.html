Five Stands, Five Recipes

Mission 08 · Hard · Financial Literacy / Mathematics (fractions) · Delayed-recall PAYOFF

Sanne's Sugar Run

Answer: 4
How to get there

This requires recalling Sanne's ratio from Mission 03 ("6 lemons for every 1 cup of sugar"), not restated here. 54 lemons ÷ 6 = 9 cups of sugar needed. Sugar comes in bags of 2.5 cups: 9 ÷ 2.5 = 3.6 bags. Since you can't buy part of a bag, round UP to 4 bags.

Why this is the only answer

The ratio (6:1) and bag size (2.5 cups) are both fixed given values; the only judgment call is rounding up (not down) since a partial bag can't be purchased, which the puzzle states explicitly, leaving one forced answer.

Mission 09 · Big Aha! · Logical Reasoning (Advanced — working backward)

The Old Championship Ledger

Answer: 20
How to get there

Profit per cup is the same for every stand: $1.00 − $0.30 = $0.70. Total profit was $63, so total cups sold by all three stands = $63 ÷ $0.70 = 90 cups. Since profit-per-cup is constant, "Sunshine's profit was double Breeze's" means Sunshine's CUPS were double Breeze's cups: S = 2B. Also T = B + 10. So S + T + B = 2B + (B+10) + B = 4B + 10 = 90, giving B = 20. Then S = 40 and T = 30 (40+30+20 = 90 ✓, and 90 × $0.70 = $63.00 ✓).

Why this is the only answer

Confirmed by brute-force search over all non-negative integer values of B satisfying both relational clues and the total-profit constraint — exactly one solution exists (B = 20).

Source / fact used

Pure logic/algebra puzzle, no outside lookup — solved and confirmed unique by direct computation.
