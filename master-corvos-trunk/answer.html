Mission 07 · Hard

The Candle Game

Answer: 1
How to get there

This is a classic subtraction game (take 1-3, last one wins). A position is "safe" for the player about to move only when it's NOT a multiple of 4 (any multiple of 4 forces a loss if the opponent plays perfectly, because whatever you take, they can always take enough to bring the round total to 4). 21 is 1 more than 20 (a multiple of 4), so taking exactly 1 candle leaves 20 — a losing position for the opponent. From then on, whatever the opponent takes (1, 2, or 3), the first player takes enough to make each round total exactly 4, guaranteeing they take the very last candle.

Why this is the only answer

Verified by computing the full win/loss table for every pile size from 0 to 21: positions 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20 are the only losing positions for the player to move, and taking 1 from 21 is the unique first move that lands the opponent on one of them (taking 2 or 3 leaves 19 or 18, both winning positions for the opponent).

Source / fact used

Self-contained combinatorial game theory puzzle (a Nim-style subtraction game), verified by full computer-checked win/loss table for piles 0 through 21.
