How to get there

Stage 2: Add the badge number planted in Mission 02 (23): 68 + 23 = 91 minutes past midnight = 1 hour 31 minutes = 01:31.

Mission 09 · Aha!

Mind the Gap

Answer: horizontal
How to get there

"Mind the gap" warns about the horizontal gap between the train doors and the platform edge, which grows on curved platforms — not a vertical height difference.

Why this is the only answer

Transport history sources are explicit that the warning concerns the horizontal gap caused by platform curvature, correcting the common assumption it's about a step down.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search, e.g. Wikipedia: Mind the gap and London Transport Museum's "Mind the Gap" blog post.
