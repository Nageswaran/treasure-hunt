Mission 02 · Easy

Weather Words

Answer: Appel
How to get there

"Wind" (wind), "lucht" (air/sky), and "storm" (storm) are all weather/air words. "Appel" means apple, a fruit — unrelated to weather.

Why this is the only answer

Three of the four words share the weather/air theme; "appel" is the only one that names a food item instead.

Source / fact used

Standard Dutch vocabulary: wind = wind, lucht = air, storm = storm, appel = apple.

Mission 08 · Hard (Stage 1 of 2)

Roadrunner and Coyote

Stage 1 answer: Hare
How to get there

In the real Hare and Hounds race, a single balloon (the "Hare") launches first, lands, and lays out a target. The other balloons (the "Hounds") launch afterward and try to drop a marker on that target.

Why this is the only answer

The rules of the race specifically and unambiguously designate the first-launching, target-laying balloon as the Hare, not the Hounds.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search on the Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta's Hare and Hounds (also called "Roadrunner and Coyotes") race format.

Mission 09 · Big Aha!

Three Legs of the Drive

Answer: 33 (km)
How to get there

Let x = distance to the launch field. The chase leg is also x, and the return leg is double the chase leg (2x). Total: x + x + 2x = 4x = 132, so x = 33.

Why this is the only answer

The three legs' relationships to each other are fully specified (equal, equal, double), so there is exactly one value of x that makes the three legs sum to 132.

Source / fact used

Self-contained algebra/working-backward puzzle; recomputed independently to confirm x = 33.
