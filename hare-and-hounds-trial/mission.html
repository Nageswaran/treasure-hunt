Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Hare and Hounds Trial

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 70-90 minutes
Today is your first day on the Skyline Chase Crew. In an hour, the town's balloon festival kicks off its biggest event — the Hare and Hounds race — and Captain Rosa Ibarra needs a certified navigator riding in her chase van. Before she'll hand you the radio, she has one more thing for you to do: pass her navigator's trial. Finish it, and you'll help chase down the winning balloon for real.
MISSION 01
EASY

Packing the Van

Captain Rosa hands you the chase van's kit bag to check before launch. Inside: a compass, a walkie-talkie, a folding step-ladder, and a paintbrush.

Which one of these does NOT belong in a chase crew's kit — the one item that would never help you find or catch up with a balloon?

Hint 1

Three of these help you navigate, communicate, or see over a fence. One of them is for painting a fence, not chasing a balloon.

MISSION 02
EASY

Weather Words

Captain Rosa quizzes every new navigator on Dutch weather words, since some of the visiting pilots only speak Dutch. Here are four Dutch words: wind, lucht, storm, appel.

Three of them are about weather or air. One of them is not — it means a fruit you'd pack for lunch, not something you'd find in the sky. Which word doesn't belong?

Hint 1

"Wind" means wind, "lucht" means air, "storm" means storm. The odd one out is a round, crunchy fruit.

💡 Did you know? The very first hot air balloon passengers ever were a rooster, a sheep, and a duck — sent up by the Montgolfier brothers at Versailles on September 19, 1783, before any human dared to fly.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

Radio Static

Captain Rosa's walkie-talkie crackles and drops to clicks — Morse code. Here's her cheat-sheet for the letters you'll need:

LetterENSV
Morse.-.......-
...   .   ...–   .   –.

Decode the message. It's the number of Captain Rosa's assigned chase-radio channel — worth remembering. Type the number as a word.

Hint 1

Each group of dots and dashes, separated by a gap, is one letter. Match each group to the key.

Hint 2

The first letter is three dots: S. What comes after S-E-V...?

📓 Agent's Notebook: Chase channel seven. Worth remembering...
MISSION 04
MEDIUM

The First Sky Sailors

Captain Rosa says every navigator should know how ballooning began. Search the internet to find the exact year the first-ever manned hot air balloon flight took place — with real human pilots aboard, not just farm animals. (Two brothers built the balloon; the pilots were Pilâtre de Rozier and the Marquis d'Arlandes.)

Type the year.

Hint 1

Try searching "first manned hot air balloon flight" — it happened a few months after the famous animal flight at Versailles.

💡 Did you know? That first human flight, on November 21, 1783, lasted about 26 minutes and carried its pilots over the rooftops of Paris.

MISSION 05
MEDIUM

Roping Off the Landing Zone

The ground crew ropes off a trapezoid-shaped safe zone for landing balloons. Here are the measurements:

The two roped sides (top and bottom) are parallel to each other. Find the total area of the landing zone in square meters.
Hint 1

For a trapezoid, add the two parallel sides together, divide by 2, then multiply by the height.

Hint 2

(40 + 26) ÷ 2 = 33. Now multiply 33 by the height.

MISSION 06
HARD

Three Spotters, One Balloon

Three ground spotters — Dana, Theo, and Mika — radio in about where a balloon landed. It's one of three fields: Miller's Pasture, Oak Ridge Field, or Sunflower Meadow.

You already know Miller's Pasture had cows grazing in it all morning — the balloon couldn't have landed there.

  • Dana says: "It landed in Oak Ridge Field."
  • Theo says: "It landed in Sunflower Meadow."
  • Mika says: "Dana is lying."

Exactly ONE of the three spotters always lies — the other two always tell the truth. Which field did the balloon actually land in?

Hint 1

Try assuming Dana is telling the truth (Oak Ridge). Check whether that makes exactly one liar among the three. Then try assuming she's lying, and check again.

Hint 2

If Dana were truthful (Oak Ridge), then Theo AND Mika would both have to be wrong — that's two liars, not one. Only one assumption gives exactly one liar.

MISSION 08
HARD

Roadrunner and Coyote

Stage 1 of 2

The real Hare and Hounds race (sometimes called "Roadrunner and Coyotes") started at a famous balloon festival in the United States. In the race, one balloon launches first and lands to lay out a target. The other balloons then launch and try to drop a marker as close to that target as possible.

Search the internet to find: which balloon in the race launches FIRST and lands to lay the target — the "Hare" or the "Hounds"? Type just that one word.

Hint 1

Search "Hare and Hounds balloon race rules" — think about which animal usually runs first in a chase.

Stage 2 of 2

Count the letters in the word you just found. That's how many liters of fuel the van uses per hour when it's chasing at full speed today. If the van chases for 3.5 hours at that rate, how many liters does it use?

Hint 1

Count the letters in your Stage 1 answer first — that number is the liters-per-hour rate.

Hint 2

Multiply that number by 3.5.

💡 Did you know? The very first event ever held at that famous balloon festival, back in 1972, was a Hare and Hounds race — the exact same kind of race you just trained for.

MISSION 09
BIG AHA!

Three Legs of the Drive

At the end of the trial run, the van's trip odometer reads 132 km for the whole day. Captain Rosa explains: "We drove the same distance to reach the launch field as we then drove chasing the balloon. Then, because of road closures, we drove all the way back the long way round — double the distance we chased."

Those three legs (drive to the field, chase, and the long way home) add up to the total of 132 km. How far was the drive TO the launch field?

Hint 1

Let the drive-to-the-field distance be x. The chase leg is also x. The way home is double the chase leg, so 2x. Add all three together.

Hint 2

x + x + 2x = 132, which means 4x = 132. Divide to find x.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

Calling It In

The balloon has landed and the scoreboard is waiting. Take the launch-field distance you worked out earlier, and double it — because the winning marker-drop team has to drive back out to it and then return, there and back. Then add the chase team's radio channel number — the one you were told to remember on your very first day.

Radio in that final number to win the Founders' Cup.

Hint 1

Take your Mission 9 answer and double it first.

Hint 2

Now add the channel number from the very first radio message you decoded.

Case closed?

Captain Rosa hands you a chase-crew patch, still warm from her pocket. "Not bad for your first morning," she says. "Chasing a balloon means going wherever the wind takes it, trusting your own two feet and a good map — that takes courage." You've earned your place riding shotgun in the chase van, for real this time.
