Mission 05 · Medium · Language (English vocabulary — odd one out)

The Editor's Red Pen

Answer: suspicious
How to get there

RELIABLE, TRUSTWORTHY, DEPENDABLE, and CREDIBLE all mean "can be believed/trusted." SUSPICIOUS means the opposite — it describes something that looks fishy or untrustworthy. It's the odd one out.

Why this is the only answer

Four of the five words are near-synonyms; only one is an antonym of the group, so there is exactly one defensible odd-one-out.
