Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Ringmaster's Riddle

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 55-70 minutes
The Aurora Circus has rolled into town for one big show, but in the rush of setting up the tent, the Ringmaster's velvet script — the only copy of tonight's grand finale trick — has gone missing. Showtime is in a few hours. The crew is too busy raising the tent to search properly, so they've called in a detective who notices things other people miss: you.
MISSION 01
EASY

The Faded Poster

Pinned outside the Ringmaster's trailer is an old poster celebrating "250 years of the modern circus," but the year it all began has faded away. Search online: in what year is the modern circus — with its ring and mixed acts — usually said to have begun, thanks to English performer Philip Astley? Type just the year.

Hint 1

Try searching "Philip Astley first modern circus year" and check the date against more than one history source.

💡 Did you know? Philip Astley's very first circus ring was about 19 meters across, but he later found that a ring 13 meters wide worked best for both the riders and the audience. Circuses still use that same size today.

MISSION 02
EASY

Tonight's Playbill

Four acts are listed on tonight's playbill: the Juggler, the Fire-Eater, the Stilt Walker, and the Trapeze Artist. Three of these acts always keep some part of themselves touching the ground during the whole performance. One act never touches the ground at all. Which act is the odd one out?

Hint 1

Think about each act one at a time: does their body (or the stilts/props under them) ever fully leave the ground?

🗒️ Agent's Notebook: Stitched inside the lining of the Ringmaster's spare top hat, you spot a number: 63. You don't know why yet, but it feels like something worth remembering.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

The Scrambled Sign

A crew member dropped a sign and its letters scattered on the ground: TGHOSTIPL. Unscramble all nine letters to find the word — it's the bright circle of light that follows a performer around the ring.

Hint 1

Look for a smaller word hiding inside the letters first, like "light" or "spot," then see what's left over.

Hint 2

The word starts with "S" and ends with "T," and is one single word, not two.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Counting the Ticket Booths

Grab a pencil for this one. Tonight's show has three ticket booths. Booth 1 sold 128 tickets. Booth 2 sold 97 tickets. Booth 3 sold 156 tickets. How many tickets were sold in total?

Hint 1

Add the three booth totals together one pair at a time — 128 + 97 first, then add 156 to that.

Hint 2

128 + 97 = 225. Now add 156 to 225.

💡 Did you know? Tightrope walkers carry a long pole not just for show — it lowers their center of gravity and slows down every wobble, giving them more time to balance before they'd actually fall.

MISSION 05
HARD

The Missing Measurement

The big top's L-shaped base needs exactly 48 meters of trim to go all the way around it — but one measurement rubbed off in the rain. Using the total and the measurements you can still read, find the missing side length.

Add up the five sides you can read, then compare that total to the full 48 meters.
Hint 1

Add up the five measurements you CAN read: 10, 4, 6, 14, and 10.

Hint 2

Those five sides add up to 44 meters. The full perimeter is 48 meters — how much is left for the missing side?

MISSION 06
HARD

Who Was Under the Big Top

Four performers were rehearsing when the script vanished: Mia, Théo, Zara, and Milo. Each one was practicing a different act: Juggler, Acrobat, Clown, and Tightrope Walker. Use these clues to work out who was doing which act:

1. Théo is the Acrobat.

2. Mia is not the Clown and not the Tightrope Walker.

3. Milo is not the Juggler.

4. Zara is not the Tightrope Walker.

Only the Tightrope Walker was up high enough to see straight down into the Ringmaster's trailer. Who was practicing the Tightrope act?

Hint 1

Clue 1 gives you Théo right away. Then use clue 2 to see what's left for Mia.

Hint 2

Once Mia's act is settled, only Zara and Milo are left for Clown and Tightrope Walker — clue 4 picks between them.

MISSION 07
BIG AHA

The Locker Room

Stage 1 of 2

Recall the number stitched inside the Ringmaster's spare top hat, back in Mission 2. Subtract it from the total number of tickets sold tonight (your Mission 4 answer). What number do you get?

Hint 1

Your Mission 2 notebook had a number in it. Your Mission 4 answer was a total number of tickets. Subtract the smaller from the larger.

Stage 2 of 2

That number is a locker code, but lockers in the crew trailer only go up to 100. Add its three digits together, then multiply that sum by 8. Which locker number do you get?

Hint 1

Take your Stage 1 answer. Add its three digits together first, then multiply that sum by 8.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

Behind Locker 96

Locker 96 creaks open — no script inside, just a strip of paper tapped out in Morse code, telling you exactly where to look next. Decode it (dot = short signal, dash = long signal) to find the place:

▬   ●▬●   ●●▬   ▬●   ▬●▬

Hint 1

Look up a Morse code chart if you need one. Each group of dots and dashes between the gaps is one letter.

Hint 2

The first letter is a single dash — that's T. The word names a place backstage where old costumes and props get stored.

Case closed?

The script had been packed away by mistake with last season's old costumes, safe in the prop trunk the whole time. Cracking this case took an unscrambled sign, a wobbly tightrope walker, a missing measurement, and a code tapped out in dots and dashes — a different kind of thinking for every single clue. That's CREATIVITY. Well done, Agent — the show goes on tonight.
