Mission 03 · Medium

Sorting the Crates

Answer: geld
How to get there

In Dutch: de tulp, de bloembol, de veiling all take "de" — but money is "het geld," a neuter ("het") noun. "Geld" is the odd one out.

Why this is the only answer

All four words were individually checked against Dutch dictionary sources: tulp, bloembol, and veiling are confirmed "de" words, while geld is confirmed a "het" word — the only one of the four.

Source / fact used

Verified via Dutch grammar/dictionary sources (woorden.org, welklidwoord.nl and equivalent lidwoord-checking sites) for each of the four words individually.

Mission 04 · Medium

Race the Clock

Answer: 40 (cents)
How to get there

The 1st price shown (80 cents) has 0 drops applied. The 9th price shown has had 8 drops applied. Each drop is 5 cents, so total drop = 8 × 5 = 40 cents. Final price = 80 − 40 = 40 cents.

Why this is the only answer

The puzzle explicitly states "9th price shown... already dropped 8 times," removing the classic off-by-one ambiguity (is the 1st shown price already one drop in, or zero?). With that stated, the arithmetic has exactly one result.

Source / fact used

The descending "Dutch auction clock" mechanism (price starts high and falls until a buyer stops it) is real and used at Royal FloraHolland — verified via web search (Thursd's "How the Auction Clock of Royal FloraHolland Works" and the Amsterdam Tulip Museum's "Dutch Clock Auctions" page). The specific starting price and drop amount here are fictional numbers invented for this puzzle, hand-recomputed to confirm 80 − (8×5) = 40.

Mission 05 · Hard

The Greenhouse Floor Plan

Answer: 250 (square meters)
How to get there

Split the L-shape into two rectangles: a 14 m × 14 m rectangle (196 m²) and a 6 m × 9 m rectangle (54 m²). Total area = 196 + 54 = 250 m².

Why this is the only answer

Every labeled dimension is mutually consistent: the two horizontal segments (14 m + 6 m) sum to the full bottom width (20 m), and the two vertical segments (5 m + 9 m) sum to the full left height (14 m) — so the diagram and the arithmetic agree, and only one area follows from the marked numbers.

Source / fact used

Self-drawn inline SVG diagram (no external image). Hand-recomputed: 14×14=196, 6×9=54, 196+54=250; cross-checked against the whole-rectangle-minus-notch method: (20×14) − (6×5) = 280 − 30 = 250. Both methods agree.

Stage 2 is only solvable using Stage 1's exact result (47) — the puzzle text explicitly references "your Stage 1 answer," so there is a genuine dependency, not just two puzzles side by side. Both steps are plain, unambiguous arithmetic with one result each.

Fully self-contained calculation chain built from Mission 1's and Mission 2's answers. Hand-recomputed: 247−200=47; (47−7)÷2=40÷2=20.
