Mission 01 · Easy

How Does a Voice Become a Broadcast?

Answer: BEDAC
How to get there

The correct real-world order is: B (DJ speaks into microphone) → E (microphone turns sound into an electrical signal) → D (transmitter turns the signal into a radio wave and the antenna sends it out) → A (the wave travels through the air) → C (a listener's radio catches the wave and turns it back into sound). That order spells B-E-D-A-C.

Why this is the only answer

Each step physically depends on the one before it — sound can't become a signal before it exists, and a wave can't be caught before it's sent. There is only one order that makes physical sense.

Source / fact used

Verified with web_search: a transmitter converts audio into a modulated radio wave, an antenna radiates it, and a receiver's antenna and tuner convert it back to sound. See Explain That Stuff — How antennas and transmitters work and PhysicsExplained.org — How Does Radio Work?.

Mission 09 · Big "Aha!" (Advanced Logical Reasoning)

Two Callers, One Truth

Answer: museum
How to get there

Assume Rick always tells the truth. Then his claim "Nadia always lies" is true, so Nadia is a liar. Nadia's claim "Rick and I are the same type" must then be false (since she lies) — meaning Rick and Nadia are NOT the same type, which is consistent (Rick=truth-teller, Nadia=liar). No contradiction, so this assignment works.

Now assume Rick always lies instead. Then his claim "Nadia always lies" is false, so Nadia tells the truth. But then Nadia's claim "Rick and I are the same type" must be true — meaning Rick and Nadia ARE the same type. That contradicts Rick=liar and Nadia=truth-teller being different types. This assignment breaks down.

So Rick is the truth-teller and Nadia is the liar. Rick's location claim (museum) is therefore true, and Nadia's claim (library) is false.

Why this is the only answer

Only one of the two truth/liar assignments avoids a logical contradiction — this is a classic solvable knights-and-knaves structure with exactly one consistent solution, re-derived independently to confirm.

Source / fact used

Pure logical deduction from the two callers' statements as given; no outside lookup used or needed.
