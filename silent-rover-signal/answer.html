Mission 01 · Easy

The First Wheels on Mars

Answer: Sojourner
How to get there

Sojourner was solar-powered and landed inside bouncing airbags as part of the Mars Pathfinder mission on July 4, 1997. Curiosity (2012) and Perseverance (2021) are both nuclear-powered (RTG) and were both lowered by a "sky crane" — a landing method Sojourner did not use. Since Sojourner is the only one matching "solar-powered + airbag landing," and it also landed first (1997, versus 2012 and 2021), it is the answer both ways.

Why this is the only answer

The two given facts (solar-powered, airbag landing) apply to exactly one of the three named rovers — Curiosity and Perseverance share the opposite traits (nuclear-powered, sky-crane landing) and are ruled out together, leaving only Sojourner.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: NASA's own history page and Space.com confirm Sojourner was solar-powered and used an airbag landing (Mars Pathfinder, July 4, 1997). NASA/JPL and Astronomy.com confirm both Curiosity and Perseverance used nuclear power and sky-crane landings, and that Sojourner's 1997 landing predates both (Curiosity 2012, Perseverance 2021).

Mission 02 · Easy

A Note From Mission Control's Dutch Engineer

Answer: de
How to get there

"Ruimte" (space) is a "de-word" in Dutch, so it is always "de ruimte," never "het ruimte."

Why this is the only answer

Dutch nouns are fixed as either de-words or het-words; "ruimte" is consistently a de-word across Dutch dictionaries and grammar references.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: multiple Dutch-language grammar/dictionary sources (including ESA's Dutch-language kids page, which uses "de ruimte" directly) confirm "de ruimte" as correct.

Mission 03 · Medium

Choosing a Rescue Landing Zone

Answer: 50 (km², Zone A)
How to get there

Zone A (circle): diameter 8 km, so radius = 4 km. Area = π × r² = 3.14 × 16 = 50.24, rounds to 50 km². Zone B (rectangle): 9 km × 5 km = 45 km². Zone A (50) is bigger than Zone B (45).

Why this is the only answer

Both areas are computed directly from the labeled diagram dimensions with no other valid reading; 50 > 45 makes Zone A the unambiguous larger zone.

Source / fact used

Pure geometry, recomputed by hand: 3.14 × 4² = 50.24 → 50; 9 × 5 = 45.

Mission 08 · Hard

The Backup-Cell Standoff

Answer: 3
How to get there

This is a Nim-style subtraction game (moves of 1-3, 21 total... here 7 cells). The winning strategy is to always leave your opponent facing a multiple of 4. Mission Control should take 3 cells first, leaving 4. Whatever the opponent takes next (1, 2, or 3), Mission Control takes the rest to bring the total taken in that round back up to 4, always leaving a multiple of 4 until 0 remains on the opponent's turn.

Why this is the only answer

Any other first move (1 or 2) leaves 6 or 5 cells — neither is a multiple of 4, letting the opponent immediately move to a multiple of 4 and take control of the win. Only taking 3 first guarantees the win.

Source / fact used

Classic, well-established Nim subtraction-game theory; the "leave a multiple of (max move + 1)" strategy is verified game-theory logic, re-derived by hand here.
