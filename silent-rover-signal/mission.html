Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Silent Rover Signal

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 80-100 minutes

Mission Control has a problem. The Mars rover Wanderer was exploring Jezero Crater when a dust storm rolled in — and now her signal has gone quiet, except for one garbled burst of static. You've just been sworn in as Junior Mission Specialist. Every clue Wanderer left behind is real science waiting to be decoded. Solve all ten mission logs, bring her signal home, and you'll uncover the one quality that got the whole team through this crisis.

MISSION 01
EASY

The First Wheels on Mars

Wanderer was named to honor NASA's three most famous Mars rovers: Sojourner, Curiosity, and Perseverance. Search to verify these two facts, then use them to reason out the answer:

Fact 1: Two of the three rovers are nuclear-powered and were lowered onto Mars by a "sky crane."
Fact 2: Only ONE of the three rovers was solar-powered and landed bouncing inside airbags instead.

That one solar-powered, airbag-landing rover is also the OLDEST of the three — it landed first. Which rover is it?

Hint 1

Search "Sojourner rover solar powered airbag landing" to confirm which rover these two facts describe.

💡 Did you know? A day on Mars (called a "sol") lasts about 24 hours and 39 minutes — just 39 minutes longer than a day on Earth.

MISSION 02
EASY

A Note From Mission Control's Dutch Engineer

Before the storm hit, Dutch engineer Femke scribbled a note in her own language: "Wanderer heeft geen contact met de ___ gemaakt" (Wanderer has made no contact with space). Is it "de ruimte" or "het ruimte" in Dutch? Type just the correct little word: de or het.

Hint 1

Most Dutch words for big, open, abstract things (like "de wereld," the world) use this same little word.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

Choosing a Rescue Landing Zone

A backup lander may need to touch down near Wanderer. Engineers marked two possible zones. Zone A is a circular crater floor. Zone B is a rectangular flat plain. Mission Control wants the BIGGER zone, for extra safety margin. Calculate both areas (use π ≈ 3.14). Which zone is bigger — type that zone's area in km², rounded to the nearest whole number.

Area of a circle = π × radius². Area of a rectangle = length × width.
Hint 1

The diameter is the full width across the circle — the radius is half of that.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Who Touched the Antenna?

Someone adjusted Wanderer's antenna dish right before the storm hit — and it may have caused the glitch. Three engineers were on shift. Two of them always tell the truth, and one always lies (you don't know which is which). Exactly one of them touched the antenna.

Priya says: "Marco touched the antenna."
Marco says: "I did not touch the antenna."
Sam says: "I touched the antenna."

Who actually touched the antenna?

Hint 1

Try assuming each person touched it, one at a time. Count how many liars that would create — only one setup gives exactly one liar.

Hint 2

If Marco touched it, both Marco AND Sam would end up lying — that's two liars, which breaks the rule. Rule that option out first.

💡 Did you know? Mars has two tiny, potato-shaped moons named Phobos and Deimos. Phobos zips around Mars so fast it orbits three times a day!

MISSION 05
MEDIUM

The Garbled Radio Burst

Through the static, Wanderer's radio sent one clear burst using the NATO phonetic alphabet — the system pilots and astronauts use to spell out words letter by letter over crackly radios. Search online for a NATO phonetic alphabet chart, then decode this burst into a single word:

DELTA — UNIFORM — NOVEMBER — ECHO

Hint 1

Each NATO code word stands for one letter — the FIRST letter of that code word. Find a chart and take the first letter of each.

📓 Agent's Notebook: Mission Control logs this decoded word as Wanderer's official callsign. Jot it down — you'll need it again later in this case.

MISSION 06
HARD

Powering Wanderer Back Up

Stage 1 of 2

✏️ Grab a pencil for this one. Wanderer's solar panels normally generate 2,800 watts of power. During the dust storm, dust blocked most of the sunlight, so the panels are only working at 35% of normal power. How many watts is Wanderer collecting right now?

Hint 1

35% means 35 out of every 100. Try converting 35% to a decimal (0.35) and multiplying.

Stage 2 of 2

Wanderer needs 4,900 watts stored up before she can safely reboot her main computer. Using the wattage she's collecting per sol (day) from Stage 1, how many FULL sols will it take to store enough power?

Hint 1

Divide the total watts needed by the watts collected per sol.

MISSION 07
HARD

Which Website Do You Trust?

Two "urgent updates" about Wanderer just showed up online. Only ONE is a real, official NASA web address. Which website address below is the genuine, official site for NASA's Mars missions? Search to check before you answer.

A) mars.nasa.gov
B) nasa-mars-live-updates.net
C) nasa.mars-tracker.info

Type the correct website address exactly as shown above.

Hint 1

Official NASA pages live on NASA's own domain. Search for "NASA Mars mission official website" and compare what you find to the three choices.

💡 Did you know? Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest known volcano in the entire solar system — over 21 kilometers tall, at least two and a half times taller than Mount Everest!

MISSION 08
HARD

The Backup-Cell Standoff

Wanderer has 7 backup power cells left to keep her heater running through the freezing Martian night. To test the safest shutdown order, Mission Control and a rival simulation AI take turns "using" the cells. On each turn, a player must use exactly 1, 2, or 3 cells. Whoever uses the LAST cell wins the simulation. Mission Control goes first.

If both sides play perfectly, how many cells should Mission Control use on the very FIRST turn to guarantee a win?

Hint 1

Think about what total you want to leave your opponent facing. If you can always leave a multiple of 4 cells for the other player, they can never win.

Hint 2

7 cells minus what number leaves exactly 4 cells behind?

MISSION 09
HARD

The Aha! Moment — Confirming the Callsign

Wanderer's console will only accept her callsign (the word you decoded back in Mission 5) if it's scrambled first: shift EVERY letter forward by 3 places in the alphabet (A becomes D, B becomes E, and so on).

Take the callsign you noted earlier, shift each letter forward by 3, and type the scrambled result.

Hint 1

Scroll back up to Mission 5's Agent's Notebook if you need to find the callsign again.

Hint 2

D+3=G, and so on for each letter. Shift all four letters the same way.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

Wanderer's Final Transmission

Wanderer is finally back online! Her last data packet lists seven single words, in this exact order:

Comet — Orbit — Umbra — Rover — Asteroid — Gravity — Eclipse

Take the FIRST letter of each word, in order, to spell out the one quality that got Mission Control through this whole crisis. What word do the letters spell?

Hint 1

Write down just the first letter of each of the seven words, in the order given.

Hint 2

You should end up with a 7-letter word describing bravery under pressure.

Case closed?

Wanderer's wheels are rolling again, and every reading is green. It took real courage to keep working the problem when the signal went quiet — just like Mission Control did, 140 million miles from home. Well done, Agent.
