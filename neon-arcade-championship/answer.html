Mission 05 · Medium · Critical Thinking & Media Literacy (compare two claims)

The Trivia Wall Argument

Answer: B
How to get there

Search "who created Pac-Man." Pac-Man was designed by Toru Iwatani and developed/published by Namco (with Midway distributing it in North America), first released in Japan in 1980. Nintendo did not make Pac-Man — Nintendo is a separate company, known instead for games like Donkey Kong and Mario.

Why this is the only answer

This is one of the most well-documented facts in video game history, confirmed identically by Wikipedia, the official Pac-Man site (pacman.com), and MoMA's collection record ("Toru Iwatani. Pac-Man. 1980"). No credible source credits Nintendo.

Verified via web search against Wikipedia ("Pac-Man", "Tōru Iwatani"), pacman.com ("History | The Official Site for Pac-Man"), and MoMA's collection page.

Mission 07 · Hard · Logical Reasoning (Intermediate — logic grid)

Four Finalists, One Afternoon

Answer: Cleo
How to get there

Full solved grid: Ava – Cosmic Defenders – 9,800. Ben – Rocket Rally – 15,750. Cleo – Barrel Boss – 21,400. Diego – Maze Muncher – 12,300.

Deduction chain: Clue 1 puts Ben on Rocket Rally or Maze Muncher. Clue 3 says Ben scored more than the Maze Muncher player, which is impossible if Ben himself is that player — so Ben plays Rocket Rally. His score must beat 12,300 (clue 2/3), but clue 4 rules out 21,400 for a Rocket Rally player, so Ben = 15,750. Among Ava/Cleo/Diego, clue 5 keeps Cleo off Maze Muncher. Testing Ava on Maze Muncher forces Diego = 21,400 and Cleo = 9,800, which breaks clue 7 (Ava < Cleo, i.e. 12,300 < 9,800 is false) — so Diego plays Maze Muncher (12,300) instead. That leaves Ava and Cleo with 9,800 and 21,400; clue 7 (Ava < Cleo) forces Ava = 9,800 and Cleo = 21,400. Finally, clue 8 (Cosmic Defenders score < Barrel Boss score) forces Ava onto Cosmic Defenders and Cleo onto Barrel Boss, since the reverse assignment would need 21,400 < 9,800, which is false.

Why this is the only answer

Each branch point above was tested against the full clue set and exactly one assignment survived at every step; the other branch produced a direct contradiction with a stated clue. Re-solved independently to confirm a single forced solution.

Source / fact used

Self-contained logic grid — solvable purely from the 8 clues given, no outside lookup.

Mission 09 · Big Aha · Logical Reasoning (Advanced — combinatorics/counting)

How Many Ways Could It Have Gone?

Answer: 24
How to get there

4 players could take gold. For each of those, 3 remaining players could take silver. For each of those, 2 remaining players could take bronze. Total orderings = 4 × 3 × 2 = 24. (This is the permutation "4 choose 3, ordered," written P(4,3).)

Why this is the only answer

Gold, silver, and bronze are distinct, ordered positions (not an unordered group), so this is a permutation problem with exactly one correct count: 24.

Source / fact used

Self-contained combinatorics puzzle — solvable purely by reasoning, no outside lookup. References the 4 named finalists already established in Mission 7, but does not require re-solving that puzzle.
