The Practice Rosette

The 4-color repeat is the only pattern that fits all 11 given items consistently; no shorter or longer repeat unit works without contradiction.

Mission 02 · Easy

The Reddest Red

Answer: Gold
How to get there

Searching "ruby red stained glass metal oxide" shows that ruby-red and cranberry glass are traditionally made by adding gold to the glass melt (copper is used for cheaper modern reds, but the puzzle explicitly rules that out).

Why this is the only answer

The clue explicitly excludes copper and iron, and specifies a metal used in "wedding rings and old coins" — gold is the one precious metal that fits both the red-glass chemistry and that description.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: "How Is Stained Glass Colored" (Scottish Stained Glass) and geology.com's "What Causes Color in Stained and Colored Glass?" — both confirm gold produces ruby-red/cranberry glass, distinct from copper's more vermilion red.

Mission 04 · Medium (Part 1 of 2)

The City of Sixteenth-Century Glass

Answer: Gouda
How to get there

Searching "Crabeth brothers stained glass Netherlands" or "Goudse Glazen" leads directly to the Sint-Janskerk in Gouda, Netherlands, home of the famous 16th-century windows painted largely by Dirck and Wouter Crabeth (1555-1571).

Why this is the only answer

The Goudse Glazen ("Gouda glass") are named after and located specifically in Gouda — no other Dutch city fits the clue.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: Wikipedia's "List of stained glass windows in the Janskerk, Gouda" and CODART's Sint-Janskerk Gouda museum guide entry, confirming the Crabeth brothers and the Gouda location.

Mission 04 · Medium (Part 2 of 2)

The City of Sixteenth-Century Glass — Dutch word

Answer: glasinlood
How to get there

SAGLINODOL is an anagram of "glasinlood" (glas-in-lood), the Dutch term for stained/leaded glass — literally "glass in lead," referring to the lead strips (came) that hold the glass pieces together.

Why this is the only answer

The scrambled letters (S,A,G,L,I,N,O,D,O,L) match exactly one real Dutch word matching the given theme hint (glass + lead) — no other valid Dutch word uses that exact letter set.

Source / fact used

Standard Dutch vocabulary term for stained/leaded glass windows (glas-in-lood / glas-in-loodraam).

Mission 09 · Big Aha!

The Oldest Glass in the World

Answer: Augsburg
How to get there

Searching "oldest stained glass window in the world" leads to Augsburg Cathedral in Bavaria, Germany, whose southern clerestory holds five prophet windows (David, Jonah, Daniel, Moses, Hosea) from the second half of the 11th century — the oldest stained glass surviving in its original complete form.

Why this is the only answer

Guinness World Records and multiple stained-glass history sources specifically credit Augsburg Cathedral with the oldest complete stained-glass windows still in their original location; older fragments exist (e.g., Jarrow, England, ~680 AD) but those are fragments, not complete windows, so they don't fit the puzzle's "five windows... prophets" description.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: Guinness World Records ("Oldest stained glass") and Scottish Stained Glass's history articles on the Augsburg Cathedral prophet windows.
