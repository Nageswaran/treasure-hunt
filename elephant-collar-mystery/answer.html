Mission 01 · Easy

Sorting the Savanna

Answer: 3
How to get there

Herbivores that live in herds among the six: Zebra, Elephant, and Wildebeest (all plant-eaters that travel in groups). Lion and Cheetah are meat-eaters (carnivores); crocodiles are meat-eaters and largely solitary.

Why this is the only answer

Only Zebra, Elephant, and Wildebeest satisfy BOTH conditions (herbivore AND herd animal) — exactly 3.

Source / fact used

General, well-established animal-classification facts (diet and social behavior of each species) — not subject to change, no search needed beyond common knowledge, cross-checked conceptually during red-team review.

Mission 02 · Easy

Sanne's Dutch Field Note

Answer: de
How to get there

"Savanne" is a de-word in Dutch, so it is always "de savanne."

Why this is the only answer

Dutch nouns are fixed as de-words or het-words; dictionaries consistently list "savanne" with "de."

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: Van Dale's dictionary entry for "savanne" and its definition text itself uses "De savanne is een..." confirming the de-article.

Mission 06 · Hard (Multi-stage)

Counting the Family Group

Stage 1 answer: 46 (elephants)
Stage 2 (final) answer: 6 (water stations)
How to get there

Stage 1: 15% of 40 = 6; 40 + 6 = 46. Stage 2: 46 ÷ 8 = 5.75, which rounds UP to 6 full stations (a partial group still needs a full station).

Why this is the only answer

Both stages follow directly from the given numbers; Stage 2 requires Stage 1's total of 46 as its dividend, so it cannot be solved without first completing Stage 1. Rounding up (not to the nearest whole) is explicitly specified in the puzzle text to avoid ambiguity.

Source / fact used

Pure math, recomputed by hand: 40 × 0.15 = 6; 40+6=46; 46/8 = 5.75 → 6.
