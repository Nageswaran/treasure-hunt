Answer: Elena
How to get there

HOHQD was made by shifting each letter of ELENA forward by 3 (E→H, L→O, E→H, N→Q, A→D). Shifting each letter of HOHQD backward by 3 recovers ELENA.

Why this is the only answer

A Caesar shift is fully reversible and deterministic — shifting HOHQD back by exactly 3 places produces exactly one word, ELENA, no other reading is possible.

The binary-to-letter decoding is fully deterministic and produces exactly one 3-letter sequence, NRT, which is a real, unique IATA airport code with one well-documented city association (Tokyo).

Mission 08 · Hard · Multi-stage (Cryptography + Mathematics/timezone) + internet research

Race Against the Clock

Stage 1 answer: 45

JSVXCJMZI was made by shifting FORTYFIVE forward by 4 (F→J, O→S, R→V, T→X, Y→C, F→J, I→M, V→Z, E→I). Shifting back by 4 recovers FORTYFIVE, i.e. the number 45.

Stage 2 (final) answer: 13:50

Departure is 23:35 Tokyo time. Boarding closes 45 minutes before that: 23:35 − 0:45 = 22:50 Tokyo local time. Tokyo is UTC+9 year-round (no daylight saving); Reykjavik is UTC+0 year-round (no daylight saving) — a fixed 9-hour difference. Converting 22:50 Tokyo time to Reykjavik time: 22:50 − 9:00 = 13:50.

Why this is the only answer

Stage 1's Caesar shift is fully reversible and produces exactly one word/number. Stage 2's calculation depends directly on that number (45) and on two verified, DST-free UTC offsets, so there is exactly one correct clock time.

Source / fact used

Both cipher shifts and the full time calculation were verified programmatically (22:50 Tokyo → 13:50 Reykjavik). Time zone facts confirmed via web search: Japan Standard Time is UTC+9 with no daylight saving (Wikipedia, "Japan Standard Time"); Iceland has observed UTC+0 year-round with no daylight saving since 1968 (Wikipedia, "Time in Iceland").

Two Agents, One Truth

How to get there

That means Silva is the liar and Cole is the truth-teller. Cole's true statement says the gate is 14.

Why this is the only answer

Checked by testing every combination of (who is truthful) × (which of the two candidate gates, 14 or 31, is correct): exactly one combination is internally consistent (Cole truthful, gate = 14). The other three combinations all produce a contradiction.

Source / fact used
