Mission 02 · Easy · Science (Geology) & Internet Research

The Secret Ingredient of the Fairy Chimneys

Answer: tuff
How to get there

Search "Cappadocia fairy chimney rock type." Sources explain the fairy chimneys are carved from tuff — a soft, porous rock formed from compacted volcanic ash — with harder cap rock (basalt, andesite, or welded ignimbrite) on top protecting the softer tuff underneath from eroding as fast.

Why this is the only answer

Geology sources consistently name "tuff" as the specific rock type. The puzzle text explicitly rules out the more general/incorrect guesses "lava" and "ash," and clarifies it's not "tufa" (an unrelated rock formed in water), narrowing it to one defensible word.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search (geologyscience.com, Much Better Adventures): fairy chimneys form through differential erosion — soft volcanic tuff erodes faster than the harder caps (basalt/andesite/ignimbrite) that protect the columns beneath them.

The Fence Around the Launch Paddock

Answer: 20 (meters)
How to get there

The three known sides are 35 m (bottom), 19 m (top), and 12 m (left): 35 + 19 + 12 = 66 m. The total perimeter is 86 m, so the missing fourth side is 86 − 66 = 20 m.

Picture and arithmetic agree, so there is exactly one correct length.

Dawn Radio Silence

Answer: red and gold
How to get there

Decoding "YZOOLLM HVEVM RH IVW ZMW TLOW" with the mirror rule (A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X, ... M↔N) gives: BALLOON SEVEN IS RED AND GOLD. The two colors named are red and gold.

Why this is the only answer

A mirror (Atbash) substitution is a fixed, reversible one-to-one mapping — there is exactly one decoding for any given ciphertext, so exactly one pair of colors results.

Mission 07 · Hard · Logical Reasoning (Intermediate — truth-tellers and liars)

Three Staff, One Missing Logbook

Answer: Elif
How to get there

Testing each person as the lone truth-teller (the other two always lying):

• If Deniz is the truth-teller: his statement "I touched it" is true, so Deniz touched it. But then Elif's statement ("Deniz touched it") would also be true — yet Elif must be lying. Contradiction.

• If Elif is the truth-teller: her statement "Deniz touched it" is true, so Deniz touched it. But then Deniz's own statement ("I touched it") would also be true — yet Deniz must be lying. Contradiction.

• If Kerem is the truth-teller: his statement "I did not touch it" is true, so Kerem is innocent. Deniz is lying, so "I touched it" is false — Deniz is innocent. Elif is lying, so "Deniz touched it" is false — consistent with Deniz being innocent. By elimination, since exactly one of the three touched the logbook and both Deniz and Kerem are cleared, Elif touched it. No contradiction anywhere.

Why this is the only answer

Only the "Kerem is the truth-teller" branch survives without contradiction; the other two branches each self-contradict immediately, so the puzzle has exactly one forced solution.

Source / fact used

Self-contained knights-and-knaves style logic puzzle; solved from the given statements only, verified by testing all three branches by hand and confirming only one is consistent.
