Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Cappadocia Sunrise Race Mystery

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 100-130 minutes

Your aunt Mira flies hot-air balloons for a living. Every year, she enters a famous dawn balloon race held in a strange, beautiful part of Turkey — a place of stone spires and hundreds of balloons rising together at sunrise.

This morning, just before launch, two things vanished from the office tent: Aunt Mira's flight-path logbook and the engraved compass medallion trophy she's raced for all season. Race officials are stalling the start, but not for long.

You're the only one on the launch field with a detective's eye. Follow every clue, and you'll find the trophy — and uncover the value that got you here before sunrise in the first place.

MISSION 01
EASY

Where in the World Is Aunt Mira?

Before you can investigate, you need to know exactly where you are. Aunt Mira's postcards describe this place in riddles:

"Tall cone-shaped rock towers rise out of the ground here. Locals call them 'fairy chimneys.' Hundreds of hot-air balloons fill the sky at dawn, every single morning, drifting between the stone spires. This region sits in central Anatolia, in Turkey. Beneath its valleys, people once carved entire cities into the rock, far underground."

Search the internet using these clues. What is the name of this famous region? (Just the region's name is fine — you can leave off "Turkey.")

Hint 1

Try searching "fairy chimneys" together with "hot air balloons Turkey" and see which region keeps coming up.

MISSION 02
EASY

The Secret Ingredient of the Fairy Chimneys

Aunt Mira's field notebook has a torn page about the stone towers beneath her balloon route:

"...this rock is soft. Wind and rivers of rain carved it easily, over millions of years. It began as ash, spat out by volcanoes long, long ago. Each tower has a harder cap of rock balanced on top, like a stone hat — that cap protects the softer rock underneath it from wearing away too fast. That's why the towers keep their strange, pointy shape..."

Search the internet to find the one-word name of this soft, ash-based volcanic rock. (It's not the same word as "lava" or "ash" — it's a different, more specific rock name.)

Hint 1

Try searching "Cappadocia fairy chimney rock type" and look for a short word, starting with the letter T, used again and again.

💡 Did you know? Cappadocia's cone-shaped rocks began forming millions of years ago, from ash spewed out by ancient volcanoes. Erosion here is incredibly slow — the rock wears away only about 2 to 3 centimeters every 1,000 years!

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

Five Balloons on the Field

Five balloons are tied down on the launch field, each with its own striped pattern:

Balloon A — pink stripe, silver stripe, pink stripe, silver stripe
Balloon B — blue stripe, green stripe, blue stripe, green stripe
Balloon C — purple stripe, purple stripe, purple stripe, purple stripe
Balloon D — orange stripe, white stripe, orange stripe, white stripe
Balloon E — teal stripe, yellow stripe, teal stripe, yellow stripe

Four of these five balloons follow the exact same kind of pattern. One balloon breaks that pattern. Which balloon is it? Type just the letter.

Hint 1

Look closely at how many different colors each balloon actually uses, not just how many stripes it has.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

The Fence Around the Launch Paddock

Grab a pencil for this one. Balloons are tied down inside a fenced paddock, shaped like the four-sided field below. Three of its fence lengths are labeled. The fourth (marked "?") is a mystery.

Officials measured the total perimeter — all four fence sides added together — at exactly 86 meters.

What is the length, in meters, of the missing "?" fence side?

Add up the three fence sides you know, then compare that total to the full perimeter.
Hint 1

The perimeter is the sum of all four sides. If you add the three known sides and subtract from the total, what's left over?

MISSION 05
MEDIUM

Aunt Mira's Checklist

Pinned to the paddock fence is a note, written in Aunt Mira's own language, Dutch. It's her pre-flight checklist:

"Checklist vóór de vlucht: de mand, de brander, het touw, de wind, de lucht. Alles is klaar voor de zonsopgang-race!"
(Roughly: "Checklist before the flight: the basket, the burner, the rope, the wind, the air. Everything is ready for the sunrise race!")

In Dutch, most nouns use the word "de." A smaller group uses "het" instead. Four of the five checklist words use "de." One uses "het." Find that one word and type it, in Dutch.

Hint 1

Say "de" and "het" out loud before each Dutch word on the list: mand, brander, touw, wind, lucht. Only one sounds right with "het."

💡 Did you know? Hot-air ballooning is over 240 years old! On 19 September 1783, a balloon built by the Montgolfier brothers carried the very first passengers ever to fly — a sheep, a duck, and a rooster — over the palace at Versailles, France.

MISSION 06
HARD

Dawn Radio Silence

Pilots keep their radios silent during the pre-dawn launch, so crews pass short notes using a mirror code instead: each letter swaps places with its mirror-partner in the alphabet. A swaps with Z, B swaps with Y, C swaps with X, and so on, all the way to M swapping with N.

A crew member hands you this note, found near the office tent:

YZOOLLM HVEVM RH IVW ZMW TLOW

Decode it letter by letter using the mirror rule above. What two colors does the decoded message say the balloon is? Type the two colors, separated by "and" (for example: "blue and green").

Agent's Notebook This decoded message names a specific balloon and its colors. That's worth remembering, Detective — the case isn't done with it yet.
Hint 1

Write the alphabet forward on one line and backward underneath it. Match each ciphered letter to the letter below it.

Hint 2

The first decoded word is a balloon's call-sign: BALLOON. The next word is a number.

MISSION 07
HARD

Three Staff, One Missing Logbook

Three launch-field staff were near the office tent this morning: Deniz, Elif, and Kerem. Everyone on the crew knows one true thing about them: exactly one of the three always tells the truth. The other two always lie. You don't know which is which — yet.

Here's what each one told you:

Deniz says: "I touched the logbook last."
Elif says: "Deniz touched the logbook last."
Kerem says: "I did not touch the logbook last."

Test each person as the possible truth-teller, one at a time, and see which choice avoids any contradiction. Who touched the logbook last?

Hint 1

Try assuming Deniz is the truth-teller first. Does that match what Elif says? Keep testing each person the same way.

Hint 2

Only one of the three people can be the truth-teller without any statement contradicting another. Once you find that person, use elimination on the remaining two suspects to find the logbook-toucher.

💡 Did you know? Beneath Cappadocia's valleys lies Derinkuyu, an ancient underground city carved deep into the soft rock. It could shelter as many as 20,000 people, along with their animals and food stores, when danger came from above.

MISSION 08
HARD

Two Spectator Deals

Grab a pencil for this one. Spectators watching the race from the hillside can pick one of two ticket packages:

Package A: a flat rate of ₺450 for the whole morning, breakfast included.
Package B: a base price of ₺150, plus ₺6 for every minute of balloon-watching time (no breakfast).

Aunt Mira's flight — from launch until landing — takes exactly 75 minutes this morning. For a 75-minute morning, which package costs less, and by how many lira?

Type your answer exactly like this example: "b by 20" (the package letter, the word "by," then the number of lira saved).

Hint 1

First work out Package B's total cost for 75 minutes: the base price plus 75 lots of the per-minute fee.

Hint 2

Compare Package B's total to Package A's flat ₺450. Whichever is smaller is the cheaper package — subtract to find the difference.

MISSION 09
BIG AHA

Working Backward From Landing

Race officials just handed you three facts about this morning's flight:

1. Every balloon must touch down at exactly 7:15 AM — that's the race rule.
2. The race route is 20 kilometers long.
3. This morning's steady wind carries balloons at 15 kilometers per hour.
4. After finishing the route, every balloon must circle the landing field for a mandatory 5-minute safety loop before touching down.

Start from the 7:15 AM landing and work backward, step by step, to figure out: what time did Aunt Mira have to launch? Type your answer like this example: "6:05 AM".

Hint 1

First find how long the 20-kilometer route itself takes at 15 kilometers per hour. Distance divided by speed gives you time.

Hint 2

Add the 5-minute safety loop to the route time to get the total time in the air. Then count that total backward from 7:15 AM.

💡 Did you know? Cappadocia's fairy chimneys began forming during a period called the Miocene to Pliocene, roughly 3 to 10 million years ago, when nearby volcanoes buried the region in thick layers of ash.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

The Torn Pages of the Logbook

Stage 1 of 2

You find three torn logbook pages stuffed in a drawer, each with a different launch entry. Only one page is the real one — the rest are old pages from other days, left behind by mistake.

Page 12: "Balloon Three — blue and silver — launched 5:52 AM."
Page 27: "Balloon Seven — teal and white — launched 5:58 AM."
Page 31: "Balloon Seven — red and gold — launched 6:01 AM."

Think back to the balloon and colors you decoded earlier in this case. Which page number matches, exactly, both the balloon number and the colors you already uncovered?

Hint 1

Two of these pages mention the same balloon number. Only one of those two also has the matching colors.

Hint 2

Scroll back to the decoded dawn radio message if you need to check the balloon number and colors again.

Stage 2 of 2 (final)

In the margin of that real page, Aunt Mira scribbled a tiny map and a compass note:

"From the launch field's north gate: walk due EAST across two fields, until you reach the old stone dovecote tower. Turn and walk due NORTH from there. Keep going until the valley walls around you turn a deep rose-pink in the morning light, and you start passing little churches carved right into the cliffs."

Search the internet for the real Cappadocia valley known for rock walls that glow rose-pink at sunrise and sunset, with old churches carved into its cliffs. What is that valley called?

Hint 1

Try searching "Cappadocia valley pink rocks sunset churches." One valley's name comes up again and again.

Hint 2

The valley's name is also the name of a flower — the same flower's color matches the rock walls at sunrise.

Case closed?

You walked onto that launch field alone, before sunrise, with nothing but a hunch and a willingness to look closely. That's courage — not the loud kind, but the quiet kind that gets up early and follows the clues anyway. Aunt Mira's trophy is found. The balloons can finally launch. Case closed, Detective.
