Mission 01 · Easy

The Mountain That Woke Up

Answer: Vesuvius
How to get there

Why this is the only answer

Only one volcano is associated with the 79 AD destruction of Pompeii; every reputable source names Vesuvius.

Answer: 39 (square meters)
How to get there

Mission 09 · Aha!

Lava, or Something Else?

Answer: ash and pumice
How to get there

Lava never reached Pompeii. The city was buried by falling pumice and ash for about 18 hours, then hit by fast-moving pyroclastic surges — but no lava flow.

Why this is the only answer

This corrects a common misconception; multiple reputable sources explicitly confirm lava did not reach Pompeii.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search, e.g. Scientific American's "How Pompeii Perished" and pompeiicity.com's "What Really Happened at Pompeii? Myths vs. Facts."
