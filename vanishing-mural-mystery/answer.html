Mission 02 · Easy · Logical Reasoning (Foundational: Classification) + Dutch Language (Internet Research)

De Kwast, Het Doek

Answer: doek
How to get there

Searching each word plus "de of het" confirms: de kwast (brush), de emmer (bucket), de ladder (ladder), and de muur (wall) are all "de" words. "Doek" (canvas), however, is a "het" word: het doek. It's the odd one out.

Why this is the only answer

Four of the five words share the same article ("de"); only "doek" takes "het" in this sense (canvas/painting surface), which is confirmed by multiple Dutch grammar reference sites, making the classification unambiguous.

Mission 04 · Medium · Art & Culture / Science (Quick-Learn: Color Theory)

The Color Wheel Rule

Answer: blue
How to get there

The taught rule: complementary colors sit directly opposite each other on the color wheel — red↔green, blue↔orange, yellow↔purple. Sam grabbed orange by mistake ("the color exactly opposite the right one"). Orange's complement is blue, so the river ribbon was meant to be painted blue.

Why this is the only answer

Each of the three primary/secondary complementary pairs is fixed and unique on the traditional color wheel used here (red-yellow-blue primaries; orange-green-purple secondaries), so orange has exactly one complementary color: blue.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search: multiple color-theory references (IxDF, DecoArt, Sessions College) confirm the traditional RYB color wheel's three complementary pairs are red/green, blue/orange, and yellow/purple.

Big Aha! (Mission 09) · Mathematics (Multi-Stage: Paint Quantity → Paint Cost)

Fixing the River, Stage by Stage

Stage 1 answer: 6
Stage 2 (final) answer: 57
How to get there

Stage 1: The river ribbon's area is 12 m × 1.5 m = 18 m². One coat needs 18 ÷ 6 = 3 liters. Two coats need 3 × 2 = 6 liters.

Stage 2: Cans hold 2.5 liters each. 6 ÷ 2.5 = 2.4, which rounds UP to 3 whole cans (partial cans can't be bought). 3 × €19 = €57.

Why this is the only answer

Both stages use fully specified numbers with one correct order of operations each. Stage 2 depends entirely on Stage 1's result, and the "round up to whole cans" rule (explicitly stated) prevents any alternate reading that would produce a different, lower total.

Source / fact used

Self-contained multi-stage arithmetic puzzle; both stages recomputed independently before publishing (12×1.5=18; 18÷6=3; 3×2=6; 6÷2.5=2.4→3 cans; 3×19=57).
