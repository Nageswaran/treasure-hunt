Internet Detective Academy · Case File

The Case of the Missing Golden Wheel

Agent briefing — read carefully before you begin
⏱ Estimated case time: 55-70 minutes
Every Friday, the town of Alkmaar holds a real cheese market where teams of carriers race wheels of cheese across the square. This year's market has a special golden-painted wheel that always opens the ceremony — and it has vanished from the weighing house overnight. The head carrier has called in a detective before the opening bell rings. Follow the clues through the market's oldest traditions to find it in time.
MISSION 01
EASY

The Weighing House

A note pinned to the empty display stand reads: "Ask at the old weighing house on the square — it's been part of this market for centuries."

Search online to find the name of the historic building on Alkmaar's market square where cheese has been officially weighed for hundreds of years. Type just the building's name (for example: Waag).

Hint 1

Try searching "Alkmaar cheese market weighing house name."

MISSION 02
EASY

Notes from the Cheese Stall

The stallholder left a list of market words with the little word "de" or "het" missing in front of each one: kaas (cheese), korting (discount), prijs (price), markt (market), and gewicht (weight).

Four of these words take "de." One is the odd one out and takes "het" instead. Which word is the odd one out?

Hint 1

Say "het" in front of each word out loud. Only one of the five will sound right.

💡 Did you know? Alkmaar's real cheese carriers each wear their own team color, so everyone on the square can tell at a glance which team is handling which wheel.

MISSION 03
MEDIUM

The Stallholder's Discount

A regular wheel of Gouda normally costs €45 at the market stall. Every Friday, the seller gives a market-day discount of 20% off. Grab a pencil for this one — how much do you pay for the wheel after the discount, in euros?

📓 Agent's Notebook: The stallholder mutters, "The golden wheel always rides on barrow number 12 — one for each month it takes to age a truly great cheese." Worth remembering!

Hint 1

First work out 20% of €45 — that's the amount saved.

Hint 2

10% of €45 is €4.50, so 20% is double that. Subtract the saving from €45.

MISSION 04
MEDIUM

Four Steps from the Waag

A carrier says the wheel was carted off toward one of four spots on the square. Starting at the Waag, walk 4 squares east and then 2 squares north on the map below. Which landmark are you standing at?

Each square on the grid is one step. East is to the right; north is toward the top of the page.
Hint 1

Start at the orange Waag square. Count 4 squares to the right first, then 2 squares up.

Hint 2

Moving right changes which column you're in; moving up changes which row. Land on the square where both match, then see which landmark sits there.

💡 Did you know? The Waag wasn't built as a weighing house at first. It started out as a chapel for a hospital, long before it was turned into the building where cheese gets weighed today.

MISSION 05
HARD

Two Carriers, One Mistake

Two carriers each wrote down what they remember. Only one of these statements can possibly be true — the other one contradicts itself:

Carrier Jansen: "I saw the golden wheel on the blue team's barrow at 9:15 — that was right after the opening bell rang at 10:00."

Carrier de Boer: "I saw it near the Waag steps at 11:30, being loaded onto a red team barrow."

Whose statement contains a mistake that makes it impossible to trust — Jansen's or de Boer's?

Hint 1

Look closely at the two times in Jansen's own statement. Can something happen at 9:15 "right after" something else happens at 10:00?

Hint 2

9:15 comes before 10:00, not after it — that's a contradiction inside a single statement, nothing to do with the other carrier.

MISSION 06
HARD

How Old Is the Guild?

De Boer's note leads to the guild house. A plaque on the wall is missing its final line. Search online to find the year the Alkmaar cheese carriers' guild (the Kaasdragersgilde) was officially founded.

In this case file, it is the year 2026. How many years old is the guild this year?

Hint 1

Try searching "Alkmaar cheese carriers guild founded year."

Hint 2

Subtract the founding year from 2026.

💡 Did you know? A real wheel of Gouda cheese weighs around 12 kilograms — about as much as a medium-sized dog.

MISSION 07
BIG AHA

Stall Number

Stage 1 of 2

Behind the guild house is a locked storage stall. Search online to find how many different colors the Alkmaar cheese carriers' guild uses for its teams in total.

Hint 1

Try searching "Alkmaar cheese carriers guild colors" — the answer is a small number.

Stage 2 of 2

The storage stall is numbered. To open it, multiply Stage 1's answer by the barrow number only insiders would know. What stall number is it?

Hint 1

Think back to the stallholder's mutter in Mission 3 — what barrow number did they mention?

Hint 2

Multiply the number of guild colors by 12.

GRAND FINALE
FINALE

The Bell Rings

You open the stall just as the market bell rings. The golden wheel is safe inside, with a note from the head carrier:

"No single carrier could run market day alone. Reds, yellows, greens, and blues each play their part — and only together does the whole market run smoothly. What's the one word for people working together toward a shared goal?"

Hint 1

Think about what four different teams need to do well together for the market to work.

Hint 2

It's a single compound word made of "team" plus another short word.

Case closed?

The golden wheel rolls out onto the square right on time, carried by all four teams together. It turns out the market's real treasure was never the cheese — it was teamwork. Well done, Agent.
