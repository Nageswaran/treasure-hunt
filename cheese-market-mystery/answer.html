Mission 01 · Easy

The Weighing House

Answer: Waag
How to get there

Search "Alkmaar cheese market weighing house name." The historic weighing building on Alkmaar's Waagplein is called the Waag — originally a hospital chapel, converted into a weighing house in the 1580s.

Why this is the only answer

"Waag" (Dutch for "weighing house/scale") is the specific, consistently used name for this building across sources — no other building on the square serves this role.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search summarizing Wikipedia's "Waag (Alkmaar)" entry and the official kaasmarkt.nl site.

Mission 02 · Easy

Notes from the Cheese Stall

Answer: gewicht
How to get there

"Kaas" (de), "korting" (de), "prijs" (de), and "markt" (de) all take "de." "Gewicht" (weight) is a neuter word and takes "het" — the odd one out.

Why this is the only answer

Each word's article was individually verified; exactly one of the five ("gewicht") takes "het" while the other four take "de."

Source / fact used

Verified via web search against Dutch grammar reference sites (welklidwoord.nl, webwoordenboek.nl) for each word: de kaas, de korting, de prijs, de markt, het gewicht.

Mission 03 · Medium

The Stallholder's Discount

Answer: 36
How to get there

20% of €45 is €9 (10% = €4.50, doubled = €9). €45 − €9 = €36.

Why this is the only answer

Fixed percentage calculation with one correct result; recomputed by hand as 45 × 0.8 = 36.

Source / fact used

Self-contained arithmetic using numbers stated in the puzzle; no external source needed.

Mission 06 · Hard

How Old Is the Guild?

Answer: 433
How to get there

Search "Alkmaar cheese carriers guild founded year." The Kaasdragersgilde (cheese carriers' guild) was founded in 1593. In the case file's stated year, 2026: 2026 − 1593 = 433.

Why this is the only answer

1593 is the specific, consistently documented founding year of the guild. The puzzle anchors to a fixed stated year (2026) rather than "this year," so the answer stays correct no matter when the mission is actually played.

Source / fact used

Verified via web search summarizing the Immaterieel Erfgoed (Dutch Intangible Heritage) entry on the Alkmaar cheese carriers' guild: founded June 17, 1593.

Grand Finale

The Bell Rings

Answer: teamwork
How to get there

The head carrier's note describes four different color teams each playing their part so that "the whole market run[s] smoothly" — a direct description of teamwork.

Why this is the only answer

Of the eight possible reward words (curiosity, courage, discovery, creativity, teamwork, kindness, wisdom, perseverance), only "teamwork" specifically describes several distinct groups combining effort toward one shared goal.

Source / fact used

Framing verified via web search on the real Alkmaar cheese carriers' guild, which is genuinely organized into four separate color teams that must coordinate for the market to run.

Reward revealed

Teamwork — the golden wheel is carried out by all four teams together, tying the real guild's four-color team structure to the reward word.
